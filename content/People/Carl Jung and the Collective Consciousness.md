I started reading "Man and his Symbols" -by Carl Jung(and others) and I got interested in this concept of how our consciousness can be shaped and how dreams can be an integral part of interpreting the unconscious. One example from the book is the dream of drunk woman that came to a man where there is difference between Jungian interpretation and Freudian Interpretation of the subconscious.

What is conscious and how is it related to the brain and how different psychologist interpret it. Sigmund Freud, for example, believed that the unconscious of each person is unique while Jung believed that we have a collective unconscious on the basis of existence of common archetypes in every culture.

The end goal is indivduation as in the total realization of the self. It can also be interpreted as integrating the conscious with the unconscious. 

Then there is the concept of different personalities developed by Jung in his early age of ego and persona. Also the analogy of ego, shadow and persona.
- ego: core of the personality. Center of consciousness and awareness.
- shadow: refers to the repressed and unconscious aspect of an individual's personality, often negative traits undesired by the society.
- persona: shown to the society. It's tha mask presented to the world
